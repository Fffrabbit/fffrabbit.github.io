## About Me

25-year-old graduate specializing in the technical side of educational technology. Through my master's thesis I explored digital disruption factors, particularly those caused by smartphones.
My interest was shaped by psychology studies during upper secondary school, and while reading research literature I noticed related psychological phenomena there.
These phenomena are brain drain, nomophobia, phone dependency, and "Fear of Missing Out." They are phone-related phenomena, and I wanted to study whether they are connected to cognitive load and academic self-regulation among IT students in higher education.

## What Is Educational Technology?

Educational technology is a field that combines information technology and pedagogy. It does not simply mean using hardware or software in teaching, but rather understanding how technology should be designed, developed, and applied so that it truly supports learning. The field is part of the Faculty of Information Technology at the University of Jyväskylä, where students have been able to major in educational technology since 2020.

In the educational technology program, students learn both practical programming skills and how to build learning environments and materials based on research evidence. As students progress through the program, they can choose one of three tracks: to become an information technology teacher in general education schools, a pedagogical specialist in educational technology, or a technical specialist in educational technology. As the name suggests, the teacher track is geared toward teaching in schools. The pedagogical specialist focuses on how technology is utilized in the planning of teaching and learning. Technical specialists, on the other hand, emphasize the development and implementation of the technology itself, such as learning environments, applications, and data analysis. I personally chose the technical specialist track because applications and data processing have always interested me the most.

A large part of the research in this field focuses on learning analytics that is, how data collected from learning and teaching situations can be analyzed and utilized to support learning. Another key area is how technology and digital skills should be taught to ensure that competencies develop effectively.

Educational technology experts find employment in a wide variety of roles: in schools and educational institutions, but equally in corporate training and development roles, in the design of learning environments and content, and in roles related to data analysis. It is precisely this combination of technical expertise and an understanding of how people actually learn that I consider to be the strength of this field and what I strive to leverage in my own work.
