# PartioMood – Redesigning an Online Safety Course

PartioMood was a four-person team project carried out in spring 2025 as part of the University of Jyväskylä's educational technology master's programme project course (Koulutusteknologian projekti, KOTES468). The client was Suomen Partiolaiset – Finlands Scouter ry (the Finnish Guides and Scouts), and the task was to redesign their mandatory "Turvallisesti yhdessä" ("Safely Together") online course — required of all adult scouts and troop leaders — in terms of content, pedagogy and technical implementation. The original course was built almost ten years earlier and no longer matched the organisation's updated safety guidelines.

## My role

I was responsible for the technical implementation: the Moodle course's technical plan and its visual/technical execution. As part of this, I independently compared two different Moodle environment versions (the University of Jyväskylä's and Suomen Partiolaiset's own) to identify compatibility issues, and documented them for the team before implementation began. I also built the project's own presentation website on the university's server.

The rest of the team: Elli-Noora Lassy (piloting and instructional videos), Johanna Mähönen (project manager, project management), and Lassi Ronkainen (pedagogical scripting). The project was supervised by Antti Ekonoja, with Leena Hiltunen as technical supervisor.

## Goals and results

The project had six goals, all either fully or partially achieved:

| Goal | Result |
|---|---|
| Course matches the updated guideline | Achieved — confirmed by the client |
| Course is meaningful to its target audience | Achieved — pilot feedback (n=61) showed 90–92% found the content easy to engage with, varied, and supportive of learning |
| Course activates participants | Achieved — 85% felt the course required applying knowledge in practice, 90% noticed receiving automatic feedback |
| Course is accessible | Achieved — technical accessibility check completed, content offered as text and audio, videos subtitled |
| Three language versions in Moodle | Partially — time ran out during the project, but instructions and materials were handed over to the client for later completion |
| Course is easy to maintain afterwards | Achieved — maintenance guide and original files delivered, maintenance training held 20 May 2025 |

The weakest single point in the pilot feedback was in-course navigation (55% agreement that progressing through the course was clear), which was addressed before launch.

The language versions were left unfinished within the project's own timeline, but the work continued afterwards: according to Suomen Partiolaiset's own article, the redesigned course launched in autumn 2025 in Finnish and Swedish, with an English version in spring 2026.

## Accessibility and AI

Accessibility was the project's second-highest priority right after content accuracy. To support it, an AI-generated (Elevenlabs) narration was produced for every text section, videos were subtitled, and content was offered in both text and audio form. This had originally been a "if time allows" goal, but the team still delivered it by prioritising accessibility over the language versions.

## Project certificate

At the end of the project, Suomen Partiolaiset's safety team and volunteer-support specialist issued a project certificate that specifically praised active sharing of perspectives, critical identification of problems, and proposing solutions while respecting the client's wishes.
